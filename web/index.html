Filming tips
  • Camera faces your chest at leg lift (RHP: 3B side, LHP: 1B side), at hip height on a tripod. Don't pan or zoom.
  • Whole body in frame, plain background, good light.
  • Use slow-mo (120–240 fps). If a slow-mo clip was exported at 30 fps, enter the real capture fps (e.g. 240).
  • Trim to set position → follow-through, or record the whole session in one video and choose "Several pitches". Same spot every session.
  • Phones are slower than laptops. Keep the screen on while it analyzes.
